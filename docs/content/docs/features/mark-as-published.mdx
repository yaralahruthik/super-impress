---
title: Mark as Published
description: Manually track your posts without connecting social accounts
---

One of the core philosophies of SuperImpress is flexibility and privacy. We understand that you might not want to connect your social media accounts directly to a new platform, or perhaps you want to track posts on platforms we don't fully support yet.

The **Mark as Published** feature allows you to manually record that a post has been published. This means you can use SuperImpress as a content drafting and organizational tool without forcing a direct integration.

## How it works

When you have a draft ready, instead of clicking "Publish" to send it through our API, you can select "Mark as Published".

1. **Select Platform**: Choose where you posted the content (e.g., LinkedIn, Twitter/X, Threads, Peerlist).
2. **Enter URL**: Paste the direct link to your live post.

This updates the status of your post in SuperImpress to "Published" and saves the link, keeping your content calendar up to date and your history organized.

## Why use this?

- **Privacy**: You don't need to grant us write access to your social profiles.
- **Throwaway Accounts**: Sign up with any email, even a throwaway one, and still get full utility from the platform.
- **Unsupported Platforms**: Track content for platforms where we haven't built an automatic integration yet.
